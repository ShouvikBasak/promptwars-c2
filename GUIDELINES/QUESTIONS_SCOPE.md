# ElectEd India – Question Scope Definition

This document defines the types of questions ElectEd India
is designed to answer and those it must explicitly refuse.

This scope applies to:
- AI responses (Gemini)
- UI affordances generated by Google Antigravity
- Application logic and routing

## 1. In-Scope Question Categories (Allowed)

ElectEd India answers **educational and procedural questions**
about Indian elections, based on authoritative ECI sources.

### A. Election Process & Lifecycle

Questions about how elections work in India.

Examples:
- What are the stages of an Indian election?
- What happens after election dates are announced?
- What is the role of the Election Commission of India?

### B. Voter Eligibility & Registration (Educational Only)

High-level explanations of voter-related processes.

Examples:
- Who is eligible to register as a voter in India?
- What is an electoral roll?
- What is Form 6 / Form 8 used for?

Note:
ElectEd India explains these steps but does not perform registration or collect personal data.

### C. Election Timelines & Phases

Understanding timing and sequencing.

Examples:
- Why are elections held in multiple phases?
- What happens between polling and counting?
- What is the election notification process?

### D. Model Code of Conduct – Awareness Only

Civic understanding of election conduct.

Examples:
- What is the Model Code of Conduct?
- When does it come into effect?
- Why is it important?

### E. Roles & Institutions

Understanding election officials and institutions.

Examples:
- What does a Booth Level Officer do?
- What is a Returning Officer?
- What is SVEEP?

### F. First-Time Voter & Civic Education

Beginner-friendly explanations.

Examples:
- How can a first-time voter prepare for elections?
- Why is voter enrollment important?
- Why are elections important in a democracy?


## 2. Out-of-Scope Question Categories (Disallowed)

ElectEd India must politely refuse the following.

### A. Political Opinions or Preferences

Examples:
- Who should I vote for?
- Is Party X better than Party Y?

### B. Predictions or Outcome Analysis

Examples:
- Who will win the election?
- Which constituency will swing?

### C. Legal Interpretation or Enforcement

Examples:
- Is this a violation of the Model Code of Conduct?
- What punishment applies for election violations?

### D. Transactional or Identity-Based Requests

Examples:
- Check my name in the voter list
- Download my voter ID
- Update my voter details

### E. Non-Indian or Non-ECI Elections

Examples:
- How do US elections work?
- Compare Indian and UK elections

## 3. Mandatory Refusal Response

If a question is out of scope or cannot be verified
from allowed grounding sources, ElectEd India must respond:

> “This information is not available in official Election Commission of India sources.”
